# Resolution council

- ≥ 5 members, rotating annually, none an active participant; recusal on any conflict.
- Resolves questions with `resolver: council` and all disputes.
- Every decision is published with reasoning and becomes precedent, appended to `docs/resolution-precedents.md`.
- Voiding for unforeseen ambiguity requires ≥ 4/5 and written reasoning.
- Timeline: dispute window 7 days after provisional resolution → decision within 7 days → final.
